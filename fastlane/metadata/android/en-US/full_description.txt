BotConnector brings cloud AI and local AI into one mobile app.

Connect your BotConnector API key to use the models available to your account, or connect compatible AI servers directly. BotConnector Local can run models from your own laptop over your local network or Tailscale when you choose to use it.

FEATURES

• Chat with BotConnector Cloud models
• Connect OpenAI-compatible remote AI servers
• Connect BotConnector Local on your own laptop
• Run supported local models directly on your phone
• Generate AI images with BotConnector image models and account quotas
• Attach photos and documents to supported BotConnector Cloud models
• Web search and tool calling when supported by the selected model and account
• Thinking/reasoning controls for compatible models
• Voice input for dictating messages
• Text-to-speech with system and optional local voice engines
• Vision input from camera or photo library
• Download and manage supported Hugging Face/GGUF local models
• Benchmark local model performance on your device
• Multiple languages, including Indonesian and English

PRIVACY AND DATA ROUTING

Local models can process supported conversations on your device. When you choose BotConnector Cloud, another remote server, web search, AI image generation, or another online feature, the relevant request leaves your device and is processed by the selected service.

Remote server credentials are stored securely on the device. The app shows remote-server privacy information before connection.

BOTCONNECTOR ACCOUNT ACCESS

Available cloud models, media models, tools, file processing, quotas, and PAYG access depend on your BotConnector account and API key. The server remains the authority for access and quota enforcement.

LOCAL AI

Local AI remains optional. You can download compatible models to the phone or connect BotConnector Local running on your own computer. Device performance and supported model size depend on available RAM, storage, and hardware acceleration.

BotConnector is developed at botconnector.id.
